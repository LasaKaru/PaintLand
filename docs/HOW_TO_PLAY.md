# How to play Inkroads

Inkroads is a road trip through living watercolour paintings. Drive a little rover along roads that loop, climb walls and run upside down, pick up the floating music notes, and explore towns on foot.

Every key below can be changed in **Menu → Settings → Controls**. The **Controls card** button on the Accessibility tab shows a printable summary.

---

## The first five minutes

1. Press **Play** on the main menu. The rover starts rolling on its own.
2. Hold **W** to speed up and steer with **A / D**.
3. Drive through the **floating notes**. They play the street's tune, and a whole run of notes **seals a phrase** ✓.
4. Press **Space** to hop. A clean landing gives a speed kick.
5. When the boost bar is full, hold **Shift** to boost.
6. Finish the lap to earn **ink** (the game's money), then press **Esc** for the menu.

> The road is your gravity: walls and ceilings are just more road. Keep driving and you stay on.

---

## Driving

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **W** / **↑** | Throttle (speed up) |
| **S** / **↓** | Brake, then reverse |
| **A D** / **← →** | Steer |
| **Space** | Hop (clean landings give a speed kick) |
| **Shift** | Boost (while the boost bar has charge) |
| **Ctrl** (left) | Drift / handbrake. Hold it through a corner and let go for a **mini-turbo** |
| **H** | Horn |
| **R** | Respawn (back on the road) |
| **L** | **Paint trail** on/off: the car leaves paint on the road. It stays there, and friends in your room see it |
| **.** / **,** | Gear up / down (only with the manual gearbox in realistic handling) |

**Tonics** are little bottles you find or buy. Each gives a power for a few seconds, with a catch:

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Q** | Drink the selected tonic |
| **Z** | Choose the next tonic |

- 🧲 **Magnet tonic:** pulls notes to you, but no boost.
- 🪶 **Feather tonic:** high jumps, but no brakes.
- 🫧 **Fizzy Ink:** 20% faster, but wobbly steering.

## On foot (in the towns)

Stop the car and press **F** (or **E**) to get out and walk. Press it again near the car to get back in.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **W A S D** | Walk |
| **Shift** | Run |
| **Ctrl** | Walk slowly |
| **Space** | Jump |
| **F** / **E** | Get in or out · talk to people · use a glowing ring |
| **G** | Emote wheel (wave, dance, cheer, sit…) |
| **Mouse** | Look around |

## Towns and glowing rings

There are four free-roam towns: **Harbour Town**, **Lantern Village**, **Serendib City** and **Tea Hills** (up the hill road from Harbour Town). A **glowing ring** on the ground is a place. Stand in it and press **E**:

- 🔧 **Garage:** vehicles, paint, parts, wraps and liveries
- 👒 **Wardrobe:** clothes, outfits and pets
- 🧪 **Shop:** tonics and extras
- 📋 **Mission board:** that town's mission stories (and a button to the chapter missions)
- 🏆 **Trophy hall:** your trophies
- 📬 **Mailbox:** postcards from friends (in Harbour Town, by your house)
- 🏡 **My home:** your house in Harbour Town: colours, keepsakes, trophy shelf, pinned postcards
- 🎨 **Paint a mural:** paint on the mural walls
- ✈ **Paper plane:** climb aboard and fly (see below)
- **→ Chapter name:** a painted gate that takes you into that chapter

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **M** | Paper map. Click a place you've discovered to travel there |

Grey districts in Serendib City are still **sketches**. Paint them back to colour by finding secrets, stunt jumps, chests and missions. Golden paint pots and **20 hidden pockets** (little scenes off the map) are tucked into corners.

## Paper-plane flight

Every town has a ✈ **Paper plane** ring with a big paper plane on a wooden stand. Stand (or park) in the ring and press **E**: a gust throws you up into the sky.

The plane has **no engine**. Dive to gain speed and pull up to trade speed for height. If you pull up too long it **stalls** and the nose drops.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **W** / **↑** | Nose down (dive, gain speed) |
| **S** / **↓** | Nose up (climb, lose speed) |
| **A D** / **← →** | Bank left / right to turn |
| **Shift** | Gust: a push forward while the gust meter lasts (it refills slowly) |
| **E** | Jump down and land where you are |
| **R** | Back to the launch ring |

- **Golden rings:** fly through all 10 in order. The clock starts at the first ring, and each town keeps your best time. The compass and minimap point to the next ring.
- **Thermals:** warm air rises over a few spots along the course and in the middle of town. Circle in one to climb.
- **Landing:** come down slowly and gently to land on your feet. Hit the ground too fast and the plane crumples, and you go back to the ring. Landing in the sea is a soggy splash.
- Fly low over a golden paint pot to pick it up.
- On a **gamepad**, the left stick flies and **X** is the gust. On a **phone**, use the stick, and **BOOST** is the gust.

## The eight chapters

Drive through a gate in a town, or choose **Menu → Chapters**:

1. **The Sketch:** the first painted town, a tower climb and loops
2. **Serendib:** Sri Lanka: Galle Face, the Lotus Tower, Sigiriya, the tea hills, the Nine Arch Bridge
3. **Wonders of the Sketchbook:** the Great Wall, the Colosseum, the Taj Mahal, Machu Picchu, Rio, Chichen Itza, Petra
4. **Lantern Roads:** Fushimi Inari, the bamboo grove, Hạ Long Bay, Hội An, the Himalaya, the Great Wave, Mount Fuji
5. **Postcards:** the Nile and the pyramids, Santorini, Kyoto by night, Kandy and Ella in the rain
6. **City Lights:** Paris, London, Venice, Amsterdam, Barcelona, Istanbul, Dubai
7. **Skylines:** New York, the Golden Gate, Rio, Tokyo, Singapore, Sydney
8. **Island Road Trip:** Colombo, the Kandy perahera, Nuwara Eliya, Ella, Yala, Galle

Each district has its own music in its own key, and the notes you collect play along on the beat.

## Photo mode

Press **P** to freeze the world and fly the camera. Press **P** or **Esc** to leave.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Drag** (mouse) | Look around |
| **W A S D** | Move the camera |
| **Space** / **Ctrl** | Camera up / down |
| **Shift** | Move faster |

In the photo panel you can:

- Set the lens, focus, blur, time, weather and art style.
- **Weather brush:** pick 🌧 rain, 🌸 petals, ✨ fireflies, ❄ snow or 🏮 lanterns, then **drag across the picture** to paint it in. Choose **Off** to look around again.
- **📷 Save PNG** (also 2× size and 4K).
- **👥 Group photo:** in a town, on foot, with friends nearby.
- **💌 Postcard:** send the picture to a friend's mailbox, or to your own.
- **🏆 Enter contest:** enter the weekly photo contest.

## Playing with friends

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enter** | Chat (in a multiplayer room) |
| **V** | Push to talk (voice chat, when turned on in Settings) |

- **Menu → Multiplayer:** join a room with friends, race each other (results are checked by the server), or play together: convoys, drift and stunt contests, and co-op paint splashes.
- **🎈 Paint battle** (Menu → Multiplayer → Play together, in any town): pink team against teal team for 2 minutes.
  - Throw paint balloons with **X** or **left click** (gamepad **RB/RT**, touch **🎈**). Hold it down to keep throwing.
  - Look up to throw further. A marker on the ground shows where the balloon will land.
  - Balloons paint the ground in your team's colour. A direct hit sends the other player back to their base for 3 seconds.
  - Your paint tank refills, and faster while you stand on your own colour.
  - The team that painted more of the ground wins. Alone or offline, bots fill both teams.
- **Menu → Account:** sign in (a name and password, no email) to keep your progress in the cloud, add friends and join a club.
- **Postcards and homes:** friends can send each other postcards and visit each other's homes in Harbour Town (Menu → Account first).
- **Mute, block and report** from the player list. Parents can lock chat, voice and online play in **Settings → Family** with a PIN.

## Things to do from the menu

- **📖 Story: The Lost Palette:** the painter Varna lost her eight colours, one in each chapter. Begin the story, then finish a lap of each chapter to bring its colour home.
- **📋 Missions** and **☀ daily brushstrokes** (keep a streak going).
- **🏁 Time trials:** ranked laps with ghosts of your best run.
- **🛣 Road Studio** (**F2** or **`**): build your own road, test drive it, and share the code.
- **🖼 Road gallery:** play and rate roads other players made. There's a weekly road contest.
- **📸 Photo contest:** a new theme every week, 3 votes each. The winner goes on the city billboards.
- **🏮 Festival games:** make a Vesak lantern (it hangs at your home), and the blindfolded New Year pot game (**◀ ▶** or **A/D** to walk, **Space** to swing).
- **📒 Sticker book**, **🏆 trophies** and the **🎟 season pass**.
- **🛠 Steam Workshop** (desktop app with Steam): subscribe to roads and liveries, and publish your own.

## Phones and tablets

Steer with the on-screen **stick** and hold **GO** to drive. The other buttons (hop, boost, drift, get out, photo, push to talk) appear on screen when you need them. Tap a glowing ring's button to use it.

## Settings worth knowing

- **Graphics:** Low, Medium, High or Ultra, a watercolour ↔ realistic art style slider, and a benchmark that picks settings for you.
- **Driving:** arcade or realistic handling, automatic or manual gearbox, steering assist, km/h or mph.
- **Accessibility:**
  - reduced motion, calm lighting, HUD size and colour-vision assist
  - chat filter and anonymous statistics (see the privacy policy)
  - seasons and festivals
  - paint-trail colour, and a button to clear your trails
- **Language:** 24 languages, chosen on the first screen or in Settings.

Have fun, and leave the roads a little more colourful than you found them. 🎨
