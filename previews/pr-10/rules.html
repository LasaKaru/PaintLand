Community Rules

Inkroads by HelaO2 · dated 2026-09-29

Inkroads is a gentle game for all ages. These rules apply everywhere players meet: rooms, chat, voice, names, the road gallery, photo contests, postcards, murals, liveries and the Steam Workshop.

Be kind

  • No bullying, threats, harassment or stalking, in the game or about the game.
  • No hate: nothing attacking people for their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, gender, sexuality, disability or age.
  • Respect a "no". If someone leaves, mutes or blocks you, let them be.

Keep it family friendly

  • No sexual content, nudity, gore, drugs or extremist symbols, in words, pictures, names or creations.
  • Nothing that sexualises or endangers children. We report this to the authorities.
  • Names, clubs and titles must be suitable for everyone.

Keep people safe

  • Never share anyone's personal details: real names, addresses, phone numbers, schools, photos of real people without permission. Protect your own too.
  • Do not ask other players, especially younger ones, to move to other apps or meet in person.
  • Do not pretend to be staff, another player, or a real person or brand.

Play fair

  • No cheating, bots, modified clients or exploiting bugs in races, contests or ranked trials. Found a bug? Tell us: we are grateful.
  • No spam, advertising, scams, or trading items for money.
  • Share only what you made or have the right to share.

How to report

  • Players: in Multiplayer, choose Report next to the player's name. Block (and muting their voice) works straight away, for you.
  • Roads and photos: use Report on the item in the gallery or contest.
  • Anything else, or urgent: write to support@helao2.com.

What happens

A person reads every report. Depending on how serious it is and whether it has happened before, we may remove the content, warn, mute, or suspend or close the account, or block access to the online features. Very serious cases can skip straight to a ban. If you think we got it wrong, write to us with your account name and we will look again.

These rules are part of the Terms of Use.
