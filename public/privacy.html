Privacy policy

Inkroads by HelaO2 · last updated 27 September 2026

Inkroads is a game. We collect as little as we can, never sell personal data, and show no third-party advertising trackers. This page lists everything the game stores. Most of it stays on your own device.

Who is responsible

HelaO2 (helao2.com). Questions and requests: support@helao2.com.

On your device only

Your progress (ink, items, looks, trophies, records, photos you save), your settings and your language are kept in your browser's or the app's local storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to use an account (below). Deleting the game's site data, or uninstalling the app, removes them.

Voice chat

Voice chat is off by default. When you turn it on, your voice goes directly to the other players (peer to peer), so they can see your internet address. We do not record voice. Blocked players are never connected to you.

Children

The game has a parental PIN (Settings → Family) that can switch off chat, voice, accounts and sharing. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond what is listed above. Accounts ask for no email or real name. If you are a parent and want something removed, write to us.

Store platforms

If you bought the game on Steam or another store, that store handles your purchase and account under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details.

Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your data. Most of it you can delete yourself: turn off statistics, delete your account, delete what you shared. For anything else (for example, a copy of your account data), email us with your account name and we will answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU/UK you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and mention it in the game's news.
